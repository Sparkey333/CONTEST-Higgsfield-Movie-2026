Before you generate anything — 16 shots are on a model that cannot do the job

The worst of them is on the ending. veo3_1 is assigned to S22 and S9. It takes a start frame only with no end frame, it caps at eight seconds against S22’s fourteen, and it does not offer 2.39:1. Three separate failures on the film’s last held close-up.

Every primary below is picked to clear all three bars at once. Follow them and the audit closes.

Camera swings, specifically

Do not reach for the preset library. All sixty-odd presets are consumer templates built around an uploaded selfie — K-pop fansign, paparazzi walk, action figure — and several take a real face as input, which the festival rules forbid outright.

Swings come from two places instead. Write the move as the whole prompt on a two-frame generation, so the model is interpolating a camera path rather than inventing a scene. And where the swing has to be repeatable or exact, transfer it: hf_mult_motion_control takes a driving clip and puts its motion onto your frames. Generate the arc once on a throwaway, then reuse it across every shot in the movement that needs the same energy.
