# Engagement Plan — 30% of the Score

**Owner: WIND 風** · **Status: 🔴 NOT STARTED — should already be running**

---

## Why this is not marketing

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Platform Engagement | 15% |
| Social Media Engagement | 15% |
| **Total** | **30%** |

Plus a separate **$100,000 Audience Choice** prize on an independent track from the
jury.

Craft criteria are contested by thousands of gifted filmmakers with brutally
non-linear returns. **Engagement is contested by almost nobody**, because most
filmmakers post once, on the last day, and hope. Effort here converts to points close
to linearly.

**Every silent day between now and Aug 31 is forfeited score.**

---

## The core move: publish the process, not just the film

We are **required** to publish prompts and generation history. Most entrants will
treat that as a compliance chore and dump a messy log on the final day.

Turn the obligation into the campaign. Higgsfield proved this works with the
**19-minute Hell Grind breakdown** — the making-of drew its own wave of coverage
independent of the film.

**The process is content. The content is scored. Post it as you make it.**

---

## Cadence — daily from today to Aug 31

| Phase | Days | What goes out |
|---|---|---|
| **Tease** | Aug 8–12 | The idea forming. Concept art, the question the film asks. Build anticipation before there is anything to show. |
| **Build** | Aug 13–23 | Real production material — character sheets, failed generations, the prompt that finally worked, before/after continuity fixes. **Failures outperform successes here.** |
| **Reveal** | Aug 24–29 | Stills, the 6-second hook, sound-design teasers. Never show the ending. |
| **Launch** | **Aug 30** | Film goes live + the full breakdown post, same day. |
| **Push** | Aug 30–31 | Audience Choice drive. Every channel. |

## The public post — a scored artifact

Not a form submission. Draft and iterate it like a shot.

- **Title** — carries the hook, not the theme
- **Thumbnail** — one frame that stops a scroll
- **Description** — logline, then the process, then the prompts
- **First comment** — pin the breakdown
- **The hook** — first 15 seconds decide Screening survival
