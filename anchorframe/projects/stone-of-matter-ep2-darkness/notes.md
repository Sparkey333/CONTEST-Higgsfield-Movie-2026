# Stone of Matter — Ep. 2 Darkness · the treatment

A short-film trailer music video, like *Matter of Light*, cut from the Episode 2 screenplay and the novel's chapters IV and VII–X; a direct sequel to the film, not only to the book. Target 5:00, board 5:24, 21:9, Cinema Studio video 4.0. Nothing is generated yet; this folder is the board before the first frame.

## Four movements
| | Movement | Shots | Time | Cue | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Stardust | S1–S6 | 1:22 | A · choral | Thousands of years ago. A light being leaves the sun of Episode 1, descends to a planet eight days old, meets the demon in the abyss of four waterfalls, and seeds the world with light. One black seed remains. |
| II | High Forest | S7–S13 | 1:20 | B · the song | Ten years after the mountain. Four friends on the lantern path; the Wind Caves concert; Ammon's spoken song — *Intelligence is light… We are one* — and the wind that answers it. |
| III | Lake of Light | S14–S18 | 1:02 | B · tail | Fireflies, the lake, the kiss under the water, and Vothek's story of the Angel Killer, told in painted light. |
| IV | The Leviticum | S19–END | 1:40 | C · dark | The library at night; movie night; the dream of Ilan and the heart-stone; the Outer Darkness — *Four of the Seven*; the elevator to the seventh floor; Master Lyon's lantern. |

## How the screenplay was compressed
- The prologue is kept whole (six shots) because it is the only part of the episode with a complete arc, and because its first shot can stand on Episode 1's elements.
- The present-day calendar talk, the race, Chalice and Maison, the class rivalry and the movie itself are dropped from the trailer; they belong to the full episode.
- The song is the spine. Its words are the screenplay's own, so the cuts in Movement II are written to them (`music.cues[1]`).
- The dream keeps three images: the woman becoming a panther, the violet consuming the emerald, the fist in the sand. The Outer Darkness keeps one line.
- The ending keeps the elevator, the voice and the lantern, and ends on the corridor — no title card inside a shot.
