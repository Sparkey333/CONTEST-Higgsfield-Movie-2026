## The Viral Script app — what it is actually good for

Three frameworks, and **all three are narrated-explainer engines**:

| Framework | What it is | For us |
|---|---|---|
| Viral Engine · 11 stages | "high-retention, story-driven viral content" | ✅ **Campaign only** |
| Speculative Asset · 8 stages | leak / asset breakdown for data dumps | ❌ Not our shape |
| Investigative Event · 7 stages | "counter-narrative engine for real-world anomalies and collective pattern debunking" | 🔴 **NEVER** — see below |

### 🔴 Compliance ruling — Catmull

**Do not use the Investigative Event framework for anything connected to this
submission.** "Counter-narrative" and "collective pattern debunking" over "real-world
anomalies" is a political-statement generator pointed at real events. The political ban
is explicit and serious violations can mean a permanent ban from future contests. There
is no version of this framework whose output is safe to attach to our entry.

### ⚠️ Why it is the wrong tool for the film itself

**D-004 locks a wordless film.** Every framework here is built to produce narration,
hooks and retention beats for a talking-head or essay video. Feed *Tidewalker* into
Viral Engine and it will hand back a voiceover script — competently — and that script
fights the exact decision that wins Catmull's vote.

If you run it on the film anyway, the background brief instructs it to return a shot
list instead, and to say so rather than producing narration. Treat anything narrated in
the output as a rejected draft, not a starting point.

### ✅ Where it earns real money

**Engagement is 30% of the rubric and the least contested surface in the contest.** This
tool is purpose-built for high-retention short-form, which is precisely what the teaser,
the process diary, and the required published-prompts breakdown are. That is Anderson's
seat, and it is worth more than any single craft criterion.

## How to fill in the screen

### 1. Framework → **Viral Engine**

### 2. Background (Optional) → **not optional for us**

Upload **[`tidewalker-background.txt`](tidewalker-background.txt)**.

The field says it exists "to prevent AI hallucinations" — that is exactly the slot for
our locks. The brief carries the ember, the turn, the subject invariants, the four-value
palette and its shift at the turn, the lens and the camera prohibition, the beat budget,
the sound spine, and every hard ban, and it opens by telling the model that the document
wins any disagreement with a draft.

**Do not upload the Aperture Method handbook here.** That is a *method* — how to develop
a film. This field wants the *story*. Uploading the method produces a script about
making films.

### 3. Your Idea → 200 characters, so it has to be exact

| # | For | Chars | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Film shot list (only with the caveat above) | 195 | Wordless 3:45 short. A sea withdraws for exactly six minutes a day. A boy runs out across the exposed seabed to retrieve something, and the water returns early. Output a shot list, not narration. |
| **B** | Teaser — the hook | 142 | The sea pulls back for exactly six minutes a day. One boy runs out onto the seabed. Why the six-minute rule exists, and what he went back for. |
| **C** | Published-prompts breakdown | 153 | How we made a 3:45 AI short: every prompt, seed and re-roll published. The character-consistency workflow that stopped the face drifting across 40 shots. |
| **D** | Process diary | 140 | Building an AI short film in 21 days for a $1,000,000 festival judged by a Pixar co-founder. What we locked before spending a single credit. |
| **E** | The method film | 148 | Why the Snowflake Method breaks for AI film, and the nine-stop method we built instead. You cannot revise a generated shot. You can only re-roll it. |

**C is the highest-value one.** We are *required* to publish prompts and generation
history — so it costs nothing extra and Higgsfield turned exactly this into a 19-minute
Hell Grind breakdown that drew its own wave of attention. Required paperwork converted
into scored content.

## The order of operations

The brief is downstream of the handbook, not a replacement for it.

1. Work **Aperture Method sheets f/1.4 → f/8** on paper. Those decisions are the locks.
2. Transcribe the locks into the background brief. *(Done for Tidewalker.)*
3. Upload the brief, generate campaign scripts.
4. Generate the **film** in Cinema Studio, from the shot ledger — not from this tool.

Never generate before the brief exists. A script tool with no grounding invents a
different film every time you run it, and you will not notice until continuity fails.
