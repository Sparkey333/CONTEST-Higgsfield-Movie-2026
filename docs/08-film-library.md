# The Short Film Library

The canonical, rankable version of this lives in the app — **[`app/index.html`](../app/index.html)**,
"The Library" room — where every title can be dragged between tiers and re-ordered. Your
ranking persists in the browser. This file is the flat mirror for reading in the repo.

**Curated against our actual problem:** a 3–5 minute, wordless-or-near-wordless,
emotionally decisive short **that never loses momentum** — judged by Catmull (heart),
Papamichael (craft), and Paul W. S. Anderson (propulsion).

> **Link honesty.** Films marked ✅ have a link verified through search this session.
> Films marked ⚠️ are widely re-uploaded and the link is a third-party copy, or the
> canonical copy sits behind Disney+/a channel index — treat the link as a starting
> point, not an official source, and never embed one in a public post of ours without
> checking it first.

Made at home for $0; became a feature. The antidote to the flashy reel with nothing
underneath.

**Piper** — Oscar winner, not one word. **This is what Catmull's taste looks like.**
Wet-sand and low-sun photography that is nearly a direct reference for *Tidewalker*.

**Paperman** — Monochrome as discipline: strip colour and everything rides on light and
shape. Hand-drawn expression fused with CG — human intent riding a machine pipeline,
which is philosophically our exact problem.

**Alike** — Colour as the narrative device itself; saturation carries the plot. A locked
palette that *shifts at the turn* reads instantly to a cinematographer juror.

**The Black Hole** — One object, one rule, greed, punchline, under three minutes. The
purest demonstration that a premise plus an ending is a complete film.

**Borrowed Time** — Oscar-nominated, made by Pixar artists after hours and deliberately
*not* Pixar-toned. Proof a hard adult subject survives in seven minutes if the images
carry it.

**Wire Cutters** — Two robots, a desolate planet, zero dialogue, a complete character
arc. Our closest reference for telling story through machines rather than faces —
directly relevant to *The Keeper*.

short clock can out-feel a feature.

**Purl** — A single visual metaphor carried all the way to its conclusion without a
line of explanation.

## The pattern across all of them

1. **One idea.** A 3–5 minute film that tries to say two things says neither.
2. **The rule is established in the first 15–20 seconds.** No preamble, ever.
3. **Almost no dialogue.** Behaviour, staging and sound carry the meaning.
4. **The turn lands at roughly 60–70%**, leaving room for the consequence to breathe.
5. **The ending recontextualises the opening.** You want to rewatch immediately.

None of them won on render quality. Every one won on a decision.

## Adding a film

Add it to the `FILMS` array in [`app/index.html`](../app/index.html) with an `id`,
`tier` (1–4), an `art` key from the `ART` map, and a `note` saying **what specifically
we are stealing from it**. A film with no stealable decision does not belong here.
Rankings already saved in a browser survive the addition — new films drop into their
default tier.

Then rebuild the published copy and re-publish it so the shared link matches:

```bash
bash scripts/build-artifact.sh
```
