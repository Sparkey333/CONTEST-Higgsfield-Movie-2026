## 1. Generation Budget

*Hell Grind* selected **253 clips from 16,181 generations** — a 1.6% keep rate, by a funded team on a 95-minute feature.

You will do better than 1.6% because your shot design is disciplined, but plan conservatively:

| | SILT | DOG | Total |
|---|---|---|---|

| Look-dev & tests | 120 | 40 | **160** |
| **Planned total** | **~910** | **~390** | **~1,300** |

**Check your credit balance against ~1,300 generations before Aug 12.** If the number doesn't work, cut DOG's shot count (it's the flexible one) rather than compromising SILT's four expensive shots.

**Where the budget actually goes.** Four shots in SILT get 3× everything: **22** (the steps reveal), **40** (the bell), **56** (the strike), **60** (the refill wide). Plus the four 85mm face shots (25, 35, 50, 58) — if those don't hold, the film doesn't exist. **Lock the character reference on those four before generating anything else.**

## 3. The Open-Source Package — Worth 15%

Your files go public after the deadline whether you curate them or not. **Curate them.** Platform Engagement is 15% of the score and this is the only lever that directly moves it.

Ship with each film:
1. **README** — logline, runtime, credits, one paragraph on the idea
2. **The Snowflake docs** — Steps 1–8, exactly as written in this repo. Almost nobody will show their story architecture. It is the most interesting thing in any of these project files.
3. **Prompt cards** — one per shot, with the locked fragments visible so people can see *how* consistency was held
4. **The Look Bible** — the three unbreakable rules, stated plainly
5. **The engineering note** — one page on forced harmonic excitation, why 82.4 Hz, and what a drawdown inspection actually is, signed with your P.E. **This is the single most differentiating artifact you can publish** and no other entrant can write it.
6. **Keep-rate data** — generations attempted vs. selected, per shot. Honest numbers. Catmull wrote a book about honest process; someone on that jury will appreciate it.

**Frame it as a tutorial, not a dump.** Title it *"How a dam engineer made a film about resonance — every prompt, and the physics."*
