## PROMPTS TO SHAKE IDEAS LOOSE

If you want to generate rather than recall, these are the angles that produce concepts fitting the
constraint set (one space, one light, one face, a turn):

**From constraint**
- One room. One person. One light source. What is the most dramatic thing that can happen?
- A job that requires absolute stillness. What interrupts it?
- Someone who cannot leave. Why not — and what is coming?

**From image**
- A light source that is also the threat *(this is Vantage)*
- A space that is being destroyed while someone lives in it normally *(this is Tenant)*
- The only light for a mile is strapped to someone's head *(this is Salvage)*
- Two thousand empty seats *(this is Ghost Light)*
- What is the most beautiful image you can think of that is also bad news?

**From truth**
- A true thing that would be cruel to say out loud
- Something you believe that you have never been able to prove
- The moment you understood your parents were people
- A time being wrong turned out to be necessary *(this is The Listening)*
- Something everyone gets wrong about a job you've done

**From structure**
- A film where the first and last shot are the same image and mean opposite things
- A film where the audience knows something no character does
- A film where the protagonist never speaks
- A film that is one continuous shot, and the single cut is the climax
- A film where what's said and what's shown disagree for four minutes
