**Note on aspect ratio:** Seedance 2.0 and FLUX 3 Video both support **21:9**, and FLUX also
supports 2:1. A wider-than-16:9 frame is a cheap, immediate signal of "film, not content" to a
jury of feature people, and it strongly favours the depth-staging and held-wide grammar in
[`../docs/02-DIRECTING-DOCTRINE.md`](../docs/02-DIRECTING-DOCTRINE.md). It also crops out a lot of
frame area where AI artifacts like to live. ⚠️ Confirm the contest permits it before committing.
