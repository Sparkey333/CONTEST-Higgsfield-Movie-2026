## The Thesis

Three jurors who want three different films:

- **Ed Catmull** wants emotional truth and a character who changes.
- **Phedon Papamichael, ASC** wants motivated light and a consistent lens.
- **Paul W. S. Anderson** wants a hook and one unforgettable set piece.

Most entrants will nail one and fail two. The single most common submission in this contest will be a fake trailer for an imaginary blockbuster, and it dies on all three.

> **The design brief: an emotionally true, simple human story, photographed with disciplined naturalistic light, inside a high-concept genre wrapper with exactly one unforgettable set piece.**

And a second thesis, which is worth as much:

> **30% of the score is not in the film.** Platform Engagement (15%) and Social Media Engagement (15%) are scored criteria. The campaign starts the day the first frame renders, not the day you submit.
