The three checks the gate turns on

Everything else on this page fails loudly — a wrong location or a wrong plate is obvious the moment you see it. These three fail quietly, and a quiet failure propagates into every frame built on top of it.

If the images do not appear. They live on the account's CDN, which a sandboxed page is not allowed to reach — so in the published view you get each frame's identity card instead. Open this file from your own copy of the repository and the real frames load. The handles, job ids and verdict capture work either way.
