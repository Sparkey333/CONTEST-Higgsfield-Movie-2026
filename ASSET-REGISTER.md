# THE SAME MIRACLE — Asset Register

Built from Chapters I–III of *Stone of Matter*. Every entry cites the source line it came from.
Status is measured against the 14 asset prompts in the director's bible.

---

## 1. What the source demands that the 14-prompt PREP set does not cover

The PREP array is built almost entirely for the light side and for people standing still. It has five characters (Gwendolyn, Torin, the brothers, the Puppeteer, the Guardians), three lighting plates, three props (shell, Stone, coral hide) and three backgrounds (courtyard, Coral Beach, temple). What it has nothing for is (a) **every effect in the film** — the void itself, which is Gwendolyn's entire power system and appears in seven shots with no reference plate; the lightning river; the vortex; the suspended shot and the floating mountain; (b) **the whole dark side** — the sixteen pursuers and their six castes, the iron ore, the ice walls, the fused Dominion wielder named in S10's own prompt, the twelve ships and their munitions; (c) **the three single largest images in the film** — the leviathan (PREP covers only a hide texture swatch, so Seedance 2.0's "identity lock via image references" in S10 has nothing to lock to), the floating mountain (S20, the shot the bible itself says "cannot be rescued in the edit"), and Proelum from orbit; (d) **the frozen tear**, which carries the S5 out-transition, the S6 match cut into Movement II, and the film's final image, on no plate at all; (e) **Guardian Isle's ground**, which carries five of the last seven shots (BG-beach is Coral Beach on Cephas — a different island — and PLATE-mv3-light is scoped "warm, peaceful, entirely still," which cannot serve the crimson, hazed, dust-filled bombardment of S17–S20); and (f) **a damaged Gwendolyn**. The source wounds her cheek (l.103), spears her left calf (l.126) and scratches her shell (l.126), and the only trained identity is a pristine studio shell — so she silently heals across the second half of Movement II. Structurally, three canon beats have no shot at all: the leviathan is never killed or freed on screen (the cut runs S11 → S12 → S13), the segmented mind — the engine of Chapter II, escalating 2→10→16→32→64→128→200+ — is never rendered, and Ammon putting his arm around his brother to die together (l.189) is cut while S22's "calculating" face, which that beat earns, is kept.

### PROPS & CREATURES

**P01 · The Leviathan — whole-creature design and scale** — PARTIAL — P0
Source: "a great leviathan ripped from the torrential depths of the abyss. Its natural plating was miraculous, covered with dead coral it had collected for years" (l.108); "its cold and cracking flesh seemed to ooze with malice" (l.110); an ordinary population, normally submerged — "She had no thought to fear the leviathans below her" (l.79). Length, head shape, eyes, teeth, colour, body plan: **not specified**. The bible's "size of a city block" (S10) and "rows of teeth" (F17) are design decisions — and city-block is materially larger than the only scale cue the source gives, which is Ch III's boys mistaking twelve tall wooden sailing ships for "legendary leviathans of the sea" (l.174). **Proposal**: settle at tall-ship-scale-plus, ~60–90m, so that line stays true and a woman running the flank for ten seconds stays legible.
Visual: deep-sea animal built like a whale crossed with an armoured fish — long, heavy, blunt-headed, dorsally ridged, longer than it is tall. Base layer of overlapping keratinous plates, wet slate-grey to grey-brown, matte where clean. Over it, years of accreted dead coral: bone-white through ash-grey, no living colour anywhere, thickest along spine and shoulder plates in strata like limescale, thinning where flesh has split, whole slabs shearing off and falling as it hangs. Seawater draining in sheets and ropes so it is uniformly soaked along the runoff lines. **Held motionless in the air — no wingbeat, no undulation, no propulsion. The stillness is the horror.** Under overcast storm light it should sit as a near-black mass with only the dead coral catching value.
Shots: S10 (16s hold), S11, S12; anchors F16, F17, B01. **S10 is assigned to Seedance 2.0 specifically for identity lock via image references at 4K, and there is no creature reference for it to lock to.**

**P02 · The void — the base unit of her power** — MISSING — P0
Source: the only direct visual the text gives — "The doubled pieces reinforced the voids surrounding the vortex, covering the entire sky with **translucent, warping bubbles**" (l.137). Behaviour: they pull and displace pressure (l.90), pull objects off course (l.115), drain her (l.136), terminate in flame at scale — "The voids exploded in spheres of flame" (l.140). Scale runs from inside a mouth (l.120) to sky-covering (l.137). Colour, edge treatment, sound: **not specified**.
Visual: **a void is a lens, not a glow.** A sphere, translucent — you see through it, but wrong. Its boundary warps and displaces the background like a lens with the wrong index: horizon lines bend, rain streaks kink where they cross the rim, the ocean behind shears. No glow, no particle system, no coloured energy — the effect is absence rendered as optical distortion, which is what makes it read as a void rather than a fireball. Air visibly rushes inward across the rim. Four studies at four scales: marble-sized inside an open mouth; a close conforming shell one hand's width off the crystal with rain deflecting in visible streamlines; body-sized freestanding over storm water with the horizon offset where it crosses; hundreds of overlapping bubbles filling an entire storm sky, cloud structure behind fractured into offset tiles.
Shots: S7, S8, S10, S11, S12, S13, S14 — more shots than any other asset, and no plate. The bible only ever says "space bends" (S8) and "a shell of bent space" (S12).

**P03 · The floating mountain, suspension, dust wall and replanting** — MISSING — P0
Source: "every projectile slowed to a halt, floating amidst the suspended particles. The shifting sands converged on the metal scraps, coming together as one large mass" (l.187); "an explosion of rock and dead coral shot from beneath the ocean waves… Dust gathered as locusts upon the rock mounds. Everything moved as sinuous waves. As the mounds engorged, they merged as one, creating what appeared to be a floating mountain" (l.190); "the mountain fell apart. It separated into countless particles, all raining down as if driven by a force stronger than gravity" (l.192); "A great dust storm stirred up, obscuring all shapes into dark-brown shadows" (l.182). Named directly: "the same unseen power that had formed the mountain" (l.193).
Visual: four plates, one material logic — everything is beach, seabed and enemy ordnance, nothing conjured. (1) SUSPENSION: iron shot and scrap frozen mid-flight against a red backlit sky, some still trailing smoke, each haloed by hanging sand grains streaming toward it in visible curved threads rather than falling. (2) ACCRETION: wet black rock and bone-white dead coral bursting up out of the sea and climbing, dust settling in dense crawling sheets like locust swarm, the mass moving in long sinuous standing waves rather than turbulence. (3) THE MOUNTAIN: a hill-sized irregular mass — not a peak, not a sphere — hanging unsupported and turning slowly, underside in deep shadow and dripping seawater, composed visibly of sand, wet rock, white coral and rust-red enemy metal, its own ordnance legible in its flank; blood-red on the west face, black on the other; its shadow falling across twelve decks. (4) COLLAPSE: converted to a million discrete particles at once, falling straight down faster than free-fall, the sea punched white — hammering the ships under rather than shattering them. Aftermath total: "No particles were left in the sky" (l.193), no wreckage, no debris. **Hard negatives throughout: no glow, no runes, no beams, no energy field, no colour shift, no air distortion, no light from the Guardians, no gesture.** Plus DUST WALL: churning brown opacity, flat dark-brown shadow, nothing legible — the film's own written-in cut point. Plus REPLANTING: craters filling from the rim inward, sand flowing uphill, stems rising and opening in seconds.
Shots: S20 (all four stages in one unbroken 14s), S19 (dust wall as opening frame and seam, B02), S21 (craters, garden). This is the shot the bible says gets the most attempts of anything in the film, and it has no study.
