Inspiration board

Eight frames that fix the look

One or two per sequence, chosen to lock the three palettes and the three camera grammars: gold on black, cold blue-grey with no warmth anywhere, then golden hour. If these read as one film, the film reads as one film.

These are look development, not production assets. The festival requires every visual asset in the film to be generated from scratch inside the submission project, and bars reuse of previously generated assets — including your own. So nothing on this page goes into the cut. It exists to make the decisions cheap: what the plasma reads as, how big the leviathan is against a person, whether the vortex works as a silence. The real frames get made again, in the project, from these decisions.

The Leviathan

Nine new assets, and the creature itself is the blocker — body turnaround, the fused rider and “the mark” that makes the weak point findable in a wide shot, the fins as a load-bearing inflation study, raw unforged ore spears, the strike, the blast, the breach column. Two of these are new beats: without them the beast is never freed, and her line has nothing behind it. Cheapest as an extension chained off S12 rather than a new cut.

Oriane — and a warning

Do not build wings, a halo, or any angelic body. “Angel of light” is a title in the source; the novel never describes an angelic form. Her elevated form is the crystal. And there are no power levels — the source's metric is understanding, and its ladder is mortal → the stages of light → able to look upon a Stone → higher being → celestialized. Godhood is explicitly relative, not a tier. The Stone of Voids is verbatim canon; the green-white colouring is invention — the word “green” appears zero times in the source.

“Drowning” — blocked, needs your call

The stem drown appears zero times in Chapters I–III, zero times in this bible, and zero times in the repository. So it is either your name for something the novel describes without naming, or it lives outside these three chapters. Three readings are possible and they share no imagery, so nothing can be built until you choose. The recommended one: the unnamed forbidden technique, which the text already describes as something you go under in — addictive, ancient temptation, submerging her in the present at the cost of the future. Under that reading it is the segmented mind plus a submersion grade: sound dropping to muffled pressure, hair and rain moving as if suspended, eyes closed, going willingly.

The Sector Guard

No armour. The word appears twice in the source and both times it is Oriane's hollow armour. The fix is not plate, it is a second layer: a heavy full-length cloak over the robe, closed down the front so each figure reads as one unbroken column at wide-shot distance. The source's summary line — “remaining shrouded in cloaks” — is quoted in this bible's own structure argument and was then dropped from the costume. There are small Keepers too, which the old sheet explicitly excluded. And the hymn is sound only: mouths closed, one still of suspended particulate and air gone dead.
