THE DOCUMENTSMaking the bible itself

Lessons from keeping the Director’s Bible and the repository, not the picture.

  • When one list drives another, audit them against each other on every change. Parity drift is silent.
  • Retire superseded work the moment its replacement exists. Keeping both is a decision left for later.
  • State what was verified and what was not, in the document itself.
  • When state looks wrong, diff against the remote before acting. The fix was a fast-forward, not a new commit.
  • Every rung of an escalation must take something as well as give. If the ladder only goes up, the ending cannot land.
  • Regenerate, don’t hand-edit. Every page is a function of its data; if the page is wrong, the data is wrong.

Cast

Does every face, place and thing exist once, by id?

On the filmDone Sep 5 · 42 sheets · 37 handles · 5 Souls (4 active) · 4 plates · 3 boards

Steps 5

Settings 4

Lessons 4

  • Never describe a covering by what it is not. The filter read the disclaimer as the subject: two credits and an hour.
  • Record old → new the moment you swap an element. Two swaps went unrecorded and four prompts pointed at dead ids.
  • Scores cannot see a wrong face. All four S5 takes carried the mortal sheet.
  • File by what a thing is, never by where it is used.

⟲Iterate · Identity. When a face moved or a shot used a superseded sheet: retire the old id, regenerate from the Soul, sweep every prompt.

GATE AIdentity lock

All sheets on one screen in the canvas. Look for a face that moved, and record the answer.

Anchors

Does the film exist as stills, in order, that are already right?

On the filmSkeleton Sep 6 · 39 anchors · 6 shared · 2 bridges · 3 match pairs

Steps 4

Settings 2

Lessons 3

  • Drift here costs an image; drift later costs a video.
  • A bridge that is only 70% black reads as a hidden cut that failed.
  • The selection pass is where the time goes, not the skeleton: one generation per frame took a day, choosing took a week.

⟲Iterate · The frame. When a composition will not survive its duration: two passes on the prompt, then rewrite.

GATE BString lock

Every frame on one contact sheet, read left to right twice. Anything that drifts is regenerated before a single video.

Motion

Is every shot a move between two frames that are already right?

On the filmSep 13 big run, 43 renders · 51 v4.0 renders Sep 10–14 · 2 of 16 chained

Steps 5

Settings 4

Lessons 4

  • The notes said v2, 12s, 720p. The account said v4.0, 16s.
  • 17 of 18 takes peaked on their last second, so the hook score undersells a slow film.
  • Regenerations with audio on invented lines (“Thank you”, “I’m sorry.”) in silent shots.
  • One generation per shot gave the editor one angle. Plan coverage or montage before Gate D.

⟲Iterate · The take. When an identity break, a missing beat or an outlier score: regenerate only that, on the right sheet, then re-score and rebuild.

GATE DMotion lock

A pick per shot, chained where it must be, no identity break in any pick.

5

Voice & sound

Is every sound in the project, and is every silence a decision?

On the filmSep 13 · 12 lines · 40 takes · 4 per line · 0.1 credit each

Steps 4

Settings 3

Lessons 3

  • Sound was the weakest region in 13 of 18 clips. Put the first line under the first three seconds.
  • A silence rule cannot survive a lyric on the climax. The vortex played under “In the sky!”.
  • The opening narration existed but was not under the black at submission.

⟲Iterate · The read. When a line reads flat or the tempo is wrong for the shot: one more take at ±12 speech rate, never more than four.

The cut

Which take, in what order, and why?

On the filmSep 14, 21:30–23:59 UTC · 22 picks · 21 favourites, 6 of them the board’s alternates

Steps 5

Settings 3

Lessons 4

  • Selection is the grade. On S22 the chroma take scored 59, the flat take 49.
  • The board planned 23 shots at 13s; the cut ran 100 scenes at 3.7s.
  • The excluded dragon takes became the 23s set piece. Show excluded takes greyed, never hidden.
  • Five Orianes and two leviathans reached the cut. Audit the timeline, not the board.

⟲Iterate · The cut. When an arc, light or hook problem against the judges: three changes, one per judge, priced in generations.

GATE EPicture lock

No further generation. The export exists and plays end to end.
