## EXT: SPACE - NIGHT

Thousands of years ago...
A blanket of STARDUST spins as the purest of whites as it
shoots through the universe of space, stepping over time,
leaving particles of dust behind to compress into stars and
planets.
The light is a source in and of itself, shape-shifting as it
desires.
Stardust, a light being, jumps through black holes to cross
great expanses of space in an instant, rapidly approaching a
destination.
The blanket of light comes to a halt. Stardust suspends
herself between the Earth and the Sun, limiting her glory so
as to not destroy the planet Proleum.
The planet Proleum is young. Eight days in celestial time,
to be exact.

## EXT: PROLEUM - CONT

A beam of particles shoots from Stardust towards the planet.
The particles converge into a small humanoid as it
approaches the atmosphere.
Her glass humanoid form is unaffected by the gravitational
pull of the planet's mass, as well as the viscous forces
from the viscous air.
Her glass figure accelerates as it approaches a massive
cloud of darkness.
Below the clouds is a desolate land drained of nutrients and
color, all centered upon a dark abyss. It's far darker than
any black hole.
Four waterfalls pour endlessly into the abyss. Their source
is still pure, though their paths have become bleak.
Everything surrounding the abyss is dead. The land and the
water.
Dim reflections of the Sun bounce from the planet only to
float about aimlessly.
Stardust's glass figure moves without hesitation. She
presses on, determined to reach as deep into the abyss as
possible.
Stardust passes with speed through the skies.
Then...she loses all motion. An abrupt halt is shattering
against her will. A power holds Stardust in place.
She faces one of the waterfalls.
A figure appears before her. A new presence.
A demon named LUCILLE.
It has four limbs and a head, though it remains crouched,
hiding teeth. It has distorted features in mockery of a man,
with hollow eyes that appear deeper black than the
surrounding darkness.
The glass surface of Stardust's body creaks and moans.
