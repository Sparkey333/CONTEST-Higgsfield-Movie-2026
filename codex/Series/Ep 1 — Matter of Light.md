From the platform's scene analysis of the master (108 scenes) read against the board:

- The cuts land on lyrics; the silent climax did not survive the song. Lock the song before the stills reel.
- Five costume states of the lead and two different beasts for one creature reached the jury: audit the timeline at Gate E, not the board.
- The Predictor refuses renders over 16s; two silent regenerations came back with phantom speech; a wide read adults as children.
- The bookend moved, the underwater coda became the ending, a 23-second dragon pair was cut as a set piece, a 9-second packshot led.
