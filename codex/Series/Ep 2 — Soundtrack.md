## 1. Every Light Casts a Shadow
*Main title · music video · cue B*

**Light makes its own shadow, and the shadow can outgrow what made it.** Ammon's spoken song at the Wind Caves: “Every light casts a shadow. This shadow, birthed of light, may be the one that finds itself larger than its creator.”

- **Fits a track:** 78–92 BPM. Starts sparse on acoustic or piano, spoken verses, the sung chorus lifts at about 1:05, a darker bridge, the biggest chorus last. The music-video form above is cut to this shape at 3:30.
- **Suno style:** `cinematic folk rock, fingerpicked acoustic intro, spoken-word verses, soaring choir chorus, building drums, minor to major lift, 84 bpm`

```
[Intro]
[Spoken Word]
Intelligence is light.
And every light casts a shadow.

[Verse 1]
[Spoken Word]
Before the world there was a word,
a promise spoken into the dark;
every glory, great or small,
was burning from that single spark.
But set a hard heart in the light
and watch the shape it throws:
a shadow born of brightness,
and how the shadow grows.

[Pre-Chorus]
So lift the lantern higher,
don't let it fall behind you...

[Chorus]
Every light casts a shadow,
every shadow knows your name;
the dark was born of brightness,
and it wants the light again.
Hold on to what you're made of,
the sun, the moon, the stars;
every light casts a shadow,
so remember who you are.

[Verse 2]
There's a black seed in the new green ground
where the wave of light came through;
there's a story of a boy who swallowed fire
and the angels that it slew.
The shadow learns to talk like you,
it says you're meant for more,
but the voice that calls you from the storm
is a voice that's lied before.

[Bridge]
Water, Voids, Dominion,
and Energy makes four;
the storm is calling names now,
and it's coming back for more.
Don't answer when it calls you,
don't reach out for its hand;
a shadow needs a light to live,
alone it cannot stand.

[Final Chorus]
Every light casts a shadow,
every shadow knows your name;
the dark was born of brightness,
and it wants the light again.
Hold on to what you're made of,
the sun, the moon, the stars;
every light casts a shadow,
so remember who you are.

[Outro]
[Spoken Word]
Creation began with a single word.
We are one.
```

## 2. We Are One

## 6. Before They Follow

- **Fits a track:** 66–80 BPM. A haunting piano or music-box opening, a whispered refrain, strings that warm into hope by the last chorus; it ends unresolved for the next episode.
