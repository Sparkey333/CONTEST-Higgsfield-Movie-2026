# Transitions between episodes

← [[_Series — MOC|Series]] · [[Legend of Litiguh — MOC]]

## How they are kept
- **A transition belongs to the episode it opens** and is listed by the episode it closes. It is written once, here, and both Anchorframe projects point at it from `project.json → bridges`.
- **In the short form** it is a 10–20 second tag after the end card of one episode and the first image of the next.
- **In the 20–60 minute form** it grows into the next episode's cold open, two to four minutes, and the tag stays as the previous episode's last beat.
- **Source pages for a transition** go into `codex/_source/` as they are, and are converted with the screenplay script, so the bridge is rewritten from the text and not from memory.

## Episode 1 → Episode 2
From the Episode 1 production screenplay (its last two pages) and the delivered film:
- Caedom alone at the courtyard's edge; the frozen tear; a crane up to a single point of light → **Ep 2 S1** lifts that point of light off the same courtyard, on the same element ids.
- The dark cloud low over the water: “It has been let in.” → **Ep 2 S3** the cloud of darkness over the abyss; **S6** the one black seed.
- The delivered film's coda, the mountain and the crater lake → **Ep 2 S7**, [[Ch 04 — Cloud Mountain|Cloud Mountain]] ten years later, with Ammon on the peak.

**Pending:** the last pages of the Episode 1 *source* screenplay (the LoL Movie Night draft, under the book's names). Attach them and they go into `_source/`, become a note in `Screenplays/`, and this bridge is rewritten from them. The Episode 1 bridge pages may also carry material for [[Ch 05 — Cephas Island|Cephas Island]] and [[Ch 06 — Interlude – the Master Levitian|the Master Levitian]], which neither episode has placed yet.

## Episode 2 → Episode 3
- Master Lyon: “You have quite a significant day approaching.” → the Trials of Guardians; Episode 3 opens on the day.
- Morningstar: Drowning “is already in pursuit of… the very Stone of Matter.” → a proposed 10–15 second tag after Episode 2's end card: water rising in the dark, somewhere.
