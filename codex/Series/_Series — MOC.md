## The ladder
World → Book → Part → Chapter → **Episode → Short film → Movement → Shot**. Shrink a chapter to a movement, a movement to a shot; grow a short to an episode by putting back what the trailer dropped (each Ep note lists it).

## Rules carried between films
What the Episode 1 read taught is written into the Episode 2 board as rules (`project.json → inherits.lessons_applied`); see [[Ep 1 — Matter of Light#What the cut taught]].
