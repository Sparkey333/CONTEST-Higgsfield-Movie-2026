# Glossary

← [[Legend of Litiguh — MOC]]

- **glisc** · 34 — both a device and a document: "Father had even installed a glisc in the floor center of their room, which projected the shifting constellations on the ceiling" and "a glisc he had read months ago."
- **Levitian / Leviticum** — one of the order / the order's seven-floor house. See [[Orders and Factions]].
- **Cephasonian** — of Cephas Island.
- **Delkai** — Ammon's family; *Son of Delkai*.
- **the Index** — the Leviticum's catalogue of plants and more.
- **the wind calls** — everyone at the Wind Caves hears the wind; what it says is different for each (screenplay).
- **the Outer Darkness** — the place with no light; also *the Outside*, where darkness is cast back to.
- **Prison of Voids** — Venetia's title as bearer of the Stone of Voids.
- **Conqueror** — Matteo's name after Ilan.
- **Litiguh** · 31 — "the killing of another Litiguh": a kind of being, and the name the series takes for its world. #verify — read the chapter.
- **Kokua** · 20 — a watchful presence; [[Ch 35 — Kokua Alone|Ch 35 — Kokua Alone]].
