# Places

← [[Legend of Litiguh — MOC]]

- **Proelum** · 63 — the world. "What followed was a millennium of peace; one-thousand years of happiness once thought impossible to exist on Proelum" ([[Ch 00 — Prologue|Ch 00 — Prologue]]). The screenplay spells it *Proleum*; eight days old in celestial time when Stardust arrives.
- **The abyss of four waterfalls** — a drained land centred on an abyss darker than any black hole, four waterfalls pouring in, pure at their sources; where Lucille waits and where Stardust's light pours out over the lip like a wave ([[Ch 09 — Song of Stardust|Ch 09 — Song of Stardust]]; screenplay prologue).
- **Cloud Mountain** · 34 · **Mirror Lake** · 15 — the peak above the lake where Ammon thinks about how the world was made ([[Ch 04 — Cloud Mountain|Ch 04 — Cloud Mountain]]); the last image of the Episode 1 film and the first present-day image of Episode 2.
- **Cephas Island** · 148 — home of the Cephasonians; the Delkai house with its damp wooden sign ([[Ch 05 — Cephas Island|Ch 05 — Cephas Island]]).
- **High Forest** · 45 — the lantern path between enormous trunks toward the Leviticum ([[Ch 07 — High Forest|Ch 07 — High Forest]]).
- **Wind Caves** · 24 — the concert in the cave mouth; the wind that calls everyone and means something different to each ([[Ch 08 — Wind Caves|Ch 08 — Wind Caves]]).
- **Lake of Light** · 21 — translucent water with limestone motes and glowing insects, trees anchored in it with high roots, a small stage against the rock wall of the North Hills ([[Ch 10 — Lake of Light|Ch 10 — Lake of Light]]).
- **The Leviticum** · 138 — the seven-floor circular library; the Index of plants; the study rooms, the glass elevator, the gold inscription above the doors ([[Ch 14 — The Leviticum|Ch 14 — The Leviticum]] and throughout).
- **Ilan** · 20 — "the hidden island of Ilan"; Matteo's; its heart is a stone ([[Ch 11 — Dark Energy|Ch 11 — Dark Energy]]).
- **Ethera** · 1 — "The island called Ethera, burrowed thousands of feet deep under bedrock" — the hidden one of Energy, found and drowned (screenplay; one mention in the book).
- **The Outer Darkness** · 12 — where there is no light; Morningstar's court; the ring of twelve.
- **Temple Center** · 3 — [[Ch 34 — Temple Center|Ch 34 — Temple Center]].
- **The Emerald Whirlpool** — [[Ch 19 — Emerald Whirlpool|Ch 19 — Emerald Whirlpool]]. **The Glass Storm** — [[Ch 27 — the Glass Storm|Ch 27 — the Glass Storm]]. #verify both.
