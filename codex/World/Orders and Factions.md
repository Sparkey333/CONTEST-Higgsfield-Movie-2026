# Orders and factions

← [[Legend of Litiguh — MOC]]

- **The Leviticum and the Levitians** · 138 / 120 — the library-order. "This was the first of many trials to becoming a Levitian." Masters Lyon, Bozolf, Kodin. The lantern of a Levitian is the sign of one.
- **The Guardians · the Sector Guard · the Trials of Guardians** · 54 / 143 — "to someday conquer the Trials of the Guardians, to join their ranks and gain their power." Guardian Iceland is Sadie's father. The final week eliminates most before the Trials begin ([[Ch 31 — the Trials Begin|Ch 31 — the Trials Begin]]).
- **The Cephasonians** — the people of Cephas Island; the House of Iceland among them.
- **The Delkai family** · 20 — Ammon's; "Father Delkai was obsessed with the cosmos"; *Son of Delkai* is what Ammon is called on stage, *Delkai spawn* what a rival calls him (screenplay).
- **Morning's followers** — Lucille (cast out with the others), Venetia, Drowning, Conqueror (Matteo), the twelve in the Outer Darkness; "Gen" banished Morning's followers (screenplay) #verify.
- **The Angel Killer** · 3 — a legend, not a faction: told every year before the Trials; "before Proelum was flooded with fire, nay, even before it was flooded with water" ([[Ch 10 — Lake of Light|Ch 10 — Lake of Light]]).
