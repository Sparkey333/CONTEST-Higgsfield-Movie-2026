# Characters

Mention counts are from the paperback text (whole-word, case-insensitive); the quoted lines are the first time the text names them. Screenplay-only facts are marked. ← [[Legend of Litiguh — MOC]]

## Ammon — the lead · 1,933
Son of the Delkai family; the adventure for his eighth birthday is where the book meets him ([[Ch 03 — Brothers|Ch 03 — Brothers]]), and "Ten years later…" he is thinking about how the world was made on a mountain peak ([[Ch 04 — Cloud Mountain|Ch 04 — Cloud Mountain]]). He hears the wind; he speaks a song rather than sings it ([[Ch 08 — Wind Caves|Ch 08 — Wind Caves]], [[Ch 09 — Song of Stardust|Ch 09 — Song of Stardust]]); the Trials of Guardians are the day approaching. Screenplay: his father is named Erik — the name does not occur in the book.

## Mikael · 449
Ammon's brother. "Ammon grinned, then shoved Mikael before taking off for the shore" ([[Ch 03 — Brothers|Ch 03 — Brothers]]). On the lantern path and at every table with him (screenplay). #verify — the book's exact relation.

## Bastion · 252
Ammon's best friend, "the largest basement of them all, harboring his own elaborate zoo" ([[Ch 07 — High Forest|Ch 07 — High Forest]] #verify). Lantern in one hand.

## Krystal · 73 · Jonah · 183
The rest of the circle: "He didn't socialize much with anyone but the Delkai family, with the addition of Krystal and Jonah." Jonah runs the glisc projector on movie night at the Leviticum. Krystal heard the wind say *Trust your true friends* (screenplay).

## Sadie · 96
"Guardian Iceland had one daughter, Sadie, whom was the same age as Ammon." Of the Cephasonian House of Iceland; grew up with him; the face he finds in the crowd at the Wind Caves and the kiss under the Lake of Light ([[Ch 10 — Lake of Light|Ch 10 — Lake of Light]]).

## Chalice · 195 · Maison · 133 · Leilani
Chalice Amber, the rival; Maison, quiet and hooded; Leilani with them (screenplay cues). Not in the trailer board; in the episode.

## Viola · Tsusani · Tank
The singer and the two brothers who play at the Wind Caves ([[Ch 08 — Wind Caves|Ch 08 — Wind Caves]]).

## Vothek the Storyteller · 7
"Every year before the Trials of Guardians, the story of the Angel Killer." Serious face, playful eyes ([[Ch 10 — Lake of Light|Ch 10 — Lake of Light]]).

## Master Lyon · 281
The Master Levitian of the interlude ([[Ch 06 — Interlude – the Master Levitian|Ch 06 — Interlude – the Master Levitian]]); the lantern on the third floor; knew Ammon's father; *tomorrow is promised to no one* (screenplay).

## Masters Bozolf · 105 · Kodin · 102
Teachers at the Leviticum; the Worlds class is theirs ([[Ch 15 — Worlds|Ch 15 — Worlds]] #verify).

## Stardust · 55
The light being of the beginning: "Stardust jumped through black holes to cross great expanses of space in an instant" — told in [[Ch 09 — Song of Stardust|Ch 09 — Song of Stardust]], the chapter the screenplay's prologue is drawn from. A blanket of light; a small humanoid of glass; she seeds Proelum and leaves.

## Lucille · 2
The dark one in the abyss: "I had hoped you would be more penitent after being cast out with the others, Lucille." Cast out; "her brother Lucille" ([[Ch 09 — Song of Stardust|Ch 09 — Song of Stardust]]). Serves Morning; reduced to nothing by Stardust's light — yet seeds of darkness remain.

## Gwendolyn · 9
"Gwendolyn stood encased in crystal, without which the Sun would incinerate her" ([[Ch 02 — Prelude – Seven Stones|Ch 02 — Prelude – Seven Stones]]). The lead of the Seven Stones prelude.

## Kokua · 20
A watchful presence: "he felt the presence of Kokua, gliding with its watchful eye." [[Ch 35 — Kokua Alone|Ch 35 — Kokua Alone]] is *Kokua Alone*. #verify — what Kokua is.

## Morningstar · 32 (and *Morning* as a name)
"The darkest and final act would soon begin, upon the release of the Morningstar." The master of the Outer Darkness; holds four of the Seven and sends for the rest ([[Ch 11 — Dark Energy|Ch 11 — Dark Energy]] #verify; screenplay).

## Venetia · 40
The Prison of Voids — the Stone of Voids — a woman who becomes a panther; she holds out the heart of Ilan.

## Matteo · 33
"His name used to be Matteo, though he was now called Conqueror." Born and raised sixteen years on the hidden island of Ilan, with a companion hog named Tash ([[Ch 11 — Dark Energy|Ch 11 — Dark Energy]]); swallows the heart of his island and kneels in the Outer Darkness.

## Drowning
The bearer of the Stone of Water; led the flood against Ethera; "already in pursuit of another, the very Stone of Matter" (screenplay).

## Cephas · 148
Cephas Island and its people, the Cephasonians ([[Ch 05 — Cephas Island|Ch 05 — Cephas Island]]); "Cephas Island would fall to the Morningstar."
