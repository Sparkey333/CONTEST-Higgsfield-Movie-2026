Five elements, named for the jury

The Council

Five persistent agents built on the Godai — the five rings Musashi used to organise mastery — each named for and aligned in spirit with a judge. Every agent argues the way its patron judges, so an internal review is a rehearsal of the real one. Their disagreement is the quality mechanism.

Catmull holds veto

No concept enters production before clearing the compliance gate. A disqualified film is worth $0 regardless of quality.

Edwin holds the spine

Phedon may change any shot. He may not lose the feeling those shots exist to produce.

Braintrust speaks last

Nothing is called finished until it has been attacked. Candor above comfort — but it must ship, not merely object.
