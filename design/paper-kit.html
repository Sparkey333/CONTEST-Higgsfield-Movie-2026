Matter of Light
THE PAPER KIT · TWELVE SHEETS FOR THINKING, NOT TRACKING

The workflow sheets record what happened; these decide what should. Structure is cheapest on paper — a re-ordered movement costs an eraser here and forty generations after Gate C. Each sheet is the paper twin of a bible step, in the same order the run sheet walks.

The loop

1Print the sheet for the step you are on. Letter, portrait, black and white is fine.
2Pencil. Thumbnail, strike out, re-order, argue in the margin. Nothing here costs a credit.
3Photograph the marked sheet — the bible's Paper panel has a notes camera that keeps the photo with the step, or drop the picture straight into the Claude session.
4Iterate. The session reads the marks, revises the prompts and the sheets, and you reprint. Each round of photo → revision is logged with the step, so the loop gets sharper as the pile grows.

The pulse — one frame every twelve seconds

Equal time between frames, whatever the shots are doing: 25 ticks across the full 5:00. Sketch what is actually on screen at each tick. This is the pacing read the shot list cannot give you — when three adjacent ticks draw the same held composition, the hold is spending 24+ seconds, and it had better be S1, S13 or S20. When every tick is a new sketch, you have built a trailer.

Every box is drawn at the width of its screen time (0.150″/s), wrapping like text. Methods — what wins now, and what this production adds

The first table is current best practice on the platform, as used by strong entrants and documented in the bible. The two boxed methods are this production's additions — one better than practice, one best available. Both were adversarially reviewed before they were written down; their qualifications are part of the method.

Image-first anchor stringdecide the film as ~39 stills before any video; motion animates decisions already made
End→start frame chainingshot N's last frame is N+1's literal first — continuity by construction, drift watched per link
Forward extensionfeed the finished clip back in and continue the take (+30s) — the oner engine, 720p tax paid at finish
Multi-shot in one generationseveral angles inside one generation hold world, wardrobe and light constant across their own cuts
Ramps and body wipesspeed ramps and near-black wipes deliver a cut's accent without leaving the frame
Motion controldrive a still character with a reference clip — choreography becomes repeatable instead of rerolled
Cheap-pass previz + promotionevery shot at budget tier first; any draft frame that beats its anchor is promoted into the ledger
Deflicker → upscale → gradein that order, always — the reverse bakes shimmer into the master permanently

BETTERThe sketch-lock loop

  1. Compose on the printed 21:9 frames (sheets 04–08) until silhouette, camera height and light direction are decided. Draw inside the frame — a photo of an unframed sketch silently re-centres when a model crops it.
  2. Photograph the winner (bible → Paper → notes camera). From then on the sketch is the shot's judging target: every batch is selected against the drawing; a re-roll that drifts is rejected on sight. Zero rules risk — nothing external enters the project.
  3. Prompt-only first. Escalate to sketch-as-reference only for stubborn layout (shot size, figure placement, the two match-cut pairs) after ~two failed batches — via the multi-reference image lane (nano_banana_pro) to build a photoreal comp draft. Never in the soul_2 lane: its one reference slot belongs to the lighting plate.
  4. When a sketch goes in: “framing, shot size and figure placement only; render photoreal; match the movement plate; no graphite, no monochrome, no paper texture.” Sketch bleed is a hard reject. Confirm on the rules page that hand-drawn reference inputs are permitted — every input publishes with the history.

Qualifications: claims precision, never credit savings — free iteration already exists on unlimited-trial models. A drawing states a composition a prompt cannot; the discipline is that the sketch stays a target by default.

Paper wins when a composition is easier to draw than to describe — stubborn framing, matched cuts, exact figure placement — and the sketch stays a target, not an upload.

BESTThe re-anchor budget

  1. Classify every join at string time: designed anchor (drift resets), inherited frame (pixel-exact start, drift-from-design accrues), or authored bridge (near-black or blinding — drift absorbed).
  2. Never more than two consecutive inherited joins. A third means promote the join to a designed anchor, or spend a bridge. This film's longest runs — S6–S8 and S10–S12 — are exactly two links; the budget makes the accident a law.
  3. Inherit start frames only — starts inherit pixel-exactly, end frames land approximately on every chainable model. Never chain onto an extracted end frame.
  4. Between resets, sub-chains are independent: strict order lives inside a sub-chain, so S6–S8, S10–S12 and the standalones can generate in parallel.
  5. At Gate D, measure each draft's landing against its designed end anchor; promote better frames in inheritance order only.

Reviewed and rejected on the way here: building a long chain from both ends toward a designed midpoint — it relocates error rather than halving it, and forces earlier shots to end on extracted frames, trading exact inheritance for approximate. The budget generalises what bridge B01 already does at S11→S12.

Phase 6 — the order that cannot be reversed

Four steps, strictly in order. Upscaling a flickering shot upscales the flicker, and there is no route back from it.

1Deflicker every extended and chained shot — the seams shimmer and this is the only pass that removes it
2Upscale to 2K/4K with the aigc preset — after deflicker, never before
3Grade — three looks, one per movement: gold-on-black · cold blue-grey · golden hour
4Gate E — picture lock. Anything still wrong is a sound problem, or it ships
